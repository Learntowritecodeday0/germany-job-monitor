# Research and chemical-employer watchlist

As of 12 November 2026. These links are official career resources, **not**
additional automatically monitored employer feeds. The dashboard has a separate
48th experimental feed for the newest three pages of the *central* Fraunhofer
portal. It cannot guarantee institute-specific or historical coverage.

| Organization | Official careers page | Relevance / caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Fraunhofer ISC | https://www.isc.fraunhofer.de/de/job-und-karriere.html | Silicates, particle technology, materials; Würzburg. Institute's full listing may not appear in recent portal pages. |
| Fraunhofer IVV | https://www.ivv.fraunhofer.de/de/jobs-karriere.html | Applied formulation, materials, packaging; Freising / Dresden. |
| Fraunhofer IGB | https://www.igb.fraunhofer.de/de/jobs-karriere.html | Interfacial and process engineering, Stuttgart / Straubing / Leuna. |
| Fraunhofer central portal | https://jobs.fraunhofer.de/search/ | Official portal; the automated monitor scans only the newest 75 results and title-filters them. |
| WACKER | https://jobs.wacker.com/ | Silicones, polymers, chemistry; register for official job alerts. Automated integration unverified. |
| Helmholtz Munich | https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/en/career/jobs | Analytical science, facilities and bioengineering; use its official job alerts. Automated feed not verified. |
| LMU Munich | https://www.lmu.de/de/die-lmu/arbeiten-an-der-lmu/stellenportal/wissenschaft/ | Scientific vacancies; formal recruitment sometimes at individual institutes. |
| University of Augsburg | https://www.uni-augsburg.de/de/jobs-und-karriere/stellenangebote/ | Research and technical positions, verify project subject and doctoral-training status. |

For selection, prioritize confirmed chemistry/materials work and real worksite.
Given past SEM Technician rejections, distinguish analytical expertise from
routine equipment operation and maintenance requirements.
